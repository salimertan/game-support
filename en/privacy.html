Privacy Notice

Updated: 21 October 2026

This notice describes account, cloud-save and controlled advertising SDK tests with authorized adult testers for Candy Pomi Match (formerly developed as Candy Ball). The game has not been released in stores. Production advertising and real purchases remain disabled; separate test installations may make real technical connections to advertising providers.

Publisher and contact

The publisher and controller of game data is Salim Ertan, an individual established in Türkiye. Support and data requests: salimertan1@gmail.com.

Device save

Unlocked levels, stars, gold, lives, boosters, quest and reward markers, tutorial state and round/level statistics are stored on your device. Language, sound, music, vibration and motion preferences stay local. You can play as a guest without creating an account.

Optional Google / Apple sign-in

The sign-in page is provided by Unity Player Accounts. You choose Google or Apple there; the game does not receive your password. The game requests authentication scope (openid), without requesting email scope. Unity may separately process an email address and unique account identifier for its account service. Google and Apple process information for their sign-in services under their own notices.

Unity Authentication authenticates your account for this game. A unique player identifier and SDK session information are used to connect the right save and maintain the session. Never email us passwords or sign-in codes.

Unity player privacy notice · Google privacy notice · Apple privacy notice

Account progress backup

After sign-in and your save selection, a copy of game progress is linked to your account through Unity Cloud Code and Cloud Save. It contains levels, stars, balances, boosters, quest/reward state and round statistics. Player identity, save revision, request identifier and save time support synchronization. Language and Pomi speech preferences are not added to the cloud copy.

The purposes are restoring your chosen account progress, reducing data loss and resolving device conflicts. Processing necessary to provide the requested service is considered on contractual-necessity grounds; security and fault handling are considered on legitimate-interest grounds where the legal conditions are met. Advertising or marketing requiring separate permission is not bundled with sign-in.

A limited number of previous revisions may be retained for recovery and conflicts. The latest copy and unresolved different copies can remain until account deletion; unnecessary older revisions are cleaned up. Deletion removes game backups and the authentication account for this game. A minimal deletion marker that prevents new saves, and provider security/backup records, may follow separate retention rules.

Signing out does not delete an account. Signing out restores the device guest backup. Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete the cloud account.

Controlled advertising tests and your choices

Test installations may update consent information through Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) and show a Turkish or English choice form where required. The form offers consent, refusal and management of options. Advertising choices are separate from account sign-in; advertising consent is not a condition for keeping your account or existing progress. The app's privacy settings can reopen advertising choices where required by the provider.

Tests may use Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and configured Google AdMob/Unity advertising services. The choice form lists the advertising partners selected for that message and links to their own notices. Depending on SDK and network settings, IP address and inferred general location, device/app details, app or device identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, performance and diagnostic data may be processed for advertising delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention. Limiting an advertising identifier does not remove all technical processing. This test does not request access to contacts, photos or precise location. Special data uses and partner choices shown in the form can be reviewed separately.

Personalization and device access that require consent depend on the applicable choices; necessary technical security processing is considered separately. A signal that an SDK can request ads does not mean consent was given for every purpose. Controlled advertising tests at this stage are limited to adult testers; production advertising will remain disabled until additional country-specific protections for younger users are completed.

Advertising reward verification

When account-linked reward testing is enabled, the server verifies the Unity session and links the player to the reward. Cloudflare Workers/D1 processes player and service-environment identifiers, a pseudonym shared with the advertising provider, request/attempt/event identifiers, reward type and timestamps. The purposes are preventing forged, duplicate or cross-account rewards and delivering an earned reward to the correct save. These pseudonyms are not anonymous data. The authentication credential is used in the verification request and is not saved in the reward database. The reward database does not request your game password, name or email address.

The reward database is configured in the EU jurisdiction. This does not mean that Unity, Google, advertising partners or all Cloudflare requests are processed only in the EU. Reward transaction records are kept to verify the test and investigate conflicts. Automatic time-based cleanup is not yet active in this test infrastructure; a completed fixed deletion period is not claimed. Account deletion removes reward attempts and event details; minimal identity digests and deletion markers that prevent replaying old rewards may remain separately. Retention limits and cleanup must be completed before production activation. You may send requests to the contact above.
