Privacy Notice

Updated: 19 October 2026

This notice covers the account and cloud-save test for the game developed under the working names Candy Ball / Pomi. Real advertising and payment services are not enabled in the test covered here.

Publisher and contact

The publisher and controller of game data is Salim Ertan, an individual established in Türkiye. Support and data requests: salimertan1@gmail.com.

Device save

Unlocked levels, stars, gold, lives, boosters, quest and reward markers, tutorial state and round/level statistics are stored on your device. Language, sound, music, vibration and motion preferences stay local. You can play as a guest without creating an account.

Optional Google / Apple sign-in

The sign-in page is provided by Unity Player Accounts. You choose Google or Apple there; the game does not receive your password. The game requests authentication scope (openid), without requesting email scope. Unity may separately process an email address and unique account identifier for its account service. Google and Apple process information for their sign-in services under their own notices.

Unity Authentication authenticates your account for this game. A unique player identifier and SDK session information are used to connect the right save and maintain the session. Never email us passwords or sign-in codes.

Unity player privacy notice · Google privacy notice · Apple privacy notice

Account progress backup

After sign-in and your save selection, a copy of game progress is linked to your account through Unity Cloud Code and Cloud Save. It contains levels, stars, balances, boosters, quest/reward state and round statistics. Player identity, save revision, request identifier and save time support synchronization. Language and Pomi speech preferences are not added to the cloud copy.

The purposes are restoring your chosen account progress, reducing data loss and resolving device conflicts. Processing necessary to provide the requested service is considered on contractual-necessity grounds; security and fault handling are considered on legitimate-interest grounds where the legal conditions are met. Advertising or marketing requiring separate permission is not bundled with sign-in.

A limited number of previous revisions may be retained for recovery and conflicts. The latest copy and unresolved different copies can remain until account deletion; unnecessary older revisions are cleaned up. Deletion removes game backups and the authentication account for this game. A minimal deletion marker that prevents new saves, and provider security/backup records, may follow separate retention rules.

Signing out does not delete an account. Signing out restores the device guest backup. Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete the cloud account.

Providers and transfers

We use Unity for authentication and cloud saves; Google or Apple for your sign-in choice; Cloudflare to serve these pages; and Gmail for support. Technical request information, such as IP address, request time and device/browser information, may be processed to operate and secure these services. International infrastructure may process data outside Türkiye or the EU. Provider agreements, appropriate transfer mechanisms and country-specific obligations still require completion before store release; this test notice does not guarantee legal compliance.

Website and support

These pages add no analytics, advertising tags, cookie-setting code, external fonts or collection forms. Cloudflare and GitHub Pages may process technical connection information for delivery and security. Cloudflare privacy notice.

Your support email address and chosen message are used to respond to your request. Do not send unrelated sensitive information, identity documents, passwords or payment details. Unnecessary correspondence is removed after resolution; records needed for a dispute or legal obligation may be retained for the applicable period.

Requests and rights
