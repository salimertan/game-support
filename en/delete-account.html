Account & Data Deletion

Updated: 21 October 2026

Delete this game account and cloud save

  1. Return to the game main menu and open Settings → Account & Progress.
  2. Sign in with Google or Apple to the account you want to delete.
  3. Select Delete Game Account and read the permanent-deletion confirmation.

This removes game backups and the Unity Authentication account for this game. The guest backup on your device is kept separately. It does not delete your Google, Apple or shared Unity Player Account. If deletion fails, the game does not report success; retry or contact support.

If account-linked advertising reward testing was used, account deletion also covers attempt and event details on the reward server. Minimal identity digests and deletion markers that prevent replaying old rewards may remain separately. Automatic time-based cleanup of these markers is not yet active in the test infrastructure. The advertising reward verification section of the privacy notice explains the data scope and current retention limitation.

If you cannot access the app

Email salimertan1@gmail.com with the subject “Game account deletion request”. Name the game and explain the access issue. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, identity documents or card information. We will use proportionate verification to identify the correct account.

Shared Unity Player Account

To separately delete Unity’s shared player account, use Unity’s account deletion page. This may affect the same Unity account used in other games; review Unity’s information.

Device-only data

Removing app data through your operating system can remove local saves and preferences; it does not delete the cloud account. Check which account holds any progress you want to keep before deleting local data.

A minimal marker preventing new saves, and provider security/backup records, may follow separate retention rules. See the privacy notice.

This is not a store release. Production advertising and real payments are disabled; separate installations may run controlled ad tests.
